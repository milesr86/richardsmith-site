How Richard's research site is put together.

Type, colour, layout, components and voice for a five-page site covering the book, the Tallersol archive, the Migration, Music and Memory project, and the publications list.

This version brings the v0.1 foundations and the proposal sent to Richard into one document, and brings both into line with the first build. The built values were read back from the live page on 16 October 2026 (register item 11), so this is now the reference rather than a set of proposals. v0.1 and the first-draft proposal move to _Superseded.

Where the look comes from

The colours are taken from screen-printed protest posters of the kind Tallersol produced: flat red and yellow ink on warm paper. The page around them stays quiet so the research is what people read, and the posters themselves, when they appear, aren't competing with the design.

Most visitors will be other academics and archivists, with some journalists and people from the communities Richard works with.

What changed in v0.2

One document now holds everything: the v0.1 foundations, the proposal's pages, voice, image rules and accessibility statement, and what the first build taught. What moved:

  1. Two boxes, named. The book (sun) and each year's publications (paper raised) are the only boxed items. Everything else is rules and space. This settles v0.1's clash between "panels and the citation box" and "not boxes".
  2. Dark palette redone. The same inks, deepened instead of lightened: red stays red, the blue stays teal, and the yellow block is a darker ochre. New fixed ink, --on-sun, for text on it. The raised panel is lifted further in dark so the citation box stands out against the page.
  3. Publication titles are 18px regular in quotes, as the proposal and v0.1's own specimen showed. The 21px semibold style is for project titles and the book.
  4. Phone menu is a burger below 640px. v0.1 said no hamburger.
  5. Footer is three lines at every size, and gains the Light / System / Dark slider.
  6. The link arrow is drawn, not typed. iOS turns the ↗ character into a blue emoji. The arrow stays on the line with its word.
  7. Line length. The 720px column stands; the 62–70 character target is dropped and logged as open item 15.
  8. Contrast recalculated for both themes and both grounds. The proposal's "about 9:1" for red on paper was wrong; it is 5.8 : 1.
  9. Pages, voice, images and accessibility from the proposal are now sections 01, 02, 07 and 09.
  10. Values read back. Register item 10 of v0.1 is closed; the differences are tabled in section 10.

01 — Pages

Five pages

Content changes a few times a year, so the site is a simple static build with no CMS. Hosting stays cheap and there is very little to break. When there is something new, Richard sends it to Miles and Miles adds it. The wordmark is the link to Home; the menu carries the other four.

Home

A sentence on what Richard works on, the book as the featured item, the two live projects and the three most recent outputs.

Research

The PhD and book, and what Richard is working on now.

Publications

Newest first, grouped by year, with DOI and repository links.

Projects

Tallersol: Memories of Resistance, and Migration, Music and Memory. This is where the poster images live.

About

The route from chemistry to history, qualifications in full, contact details, and links to ORCID, Scholar, Academia, Bluesky and LinkedIn.

A Talks or Teaching page can be added later if there is enough to fill it. Shell widths and what each page holds are in section 07.

02 — Voice

Writing for the site

  • The site speaks as Richard, in the first person, in plain British English with short paragraphs.
  • Spanish names keep their accents (Tallersol, Universidad de Chile, Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile) and are marked up with lang="es" so screen readers pronounce them properly.
  • Name the collaborators wherever they come up: Professor Sara Cohen and Professor Lisa Shaw, Tallersol, Merseyside's Chilean community. So much of the current work is joint that leaving them out would give the wrong picture.
  • Book titles in italics. Article titles in plain text with quotation marks. Journal names in the muted colour, in the meta line.
  • No emoji or exclamation marks. Avoid "passionate about".

The site launches in English with a short Spanish summary on About. Whether a full Spanish version is worth it is Richard's call (register item 12).

Use

  • Ink on paper in both themes. It always takes the text colour, never the accent.
  • The initials RGS on a sun square for the favicon and social avatar, where the full name won't fit.

04 — Colour

Palette

Warm paper and ink, one red accent, a blue that belongs to the music project, and a yellow that's only ever a fill. Every colour has a dark-theme value, and the site follows the visitor's light or dark setting. Each chip shows light on the left and dark on the right.

Every page sits on the paper colour, never pure white. Red is the only real accent and is used sparingly: links, the current menu item and year markers. Blue belongs to the music and memory project and to topic tags. Yellow appears as a fill at most once a page, with dark text on it.

In dark mode the same inks are kept but deepened, not lightened. Red stays red, the blue stays teal, and the yellow block turns a darker ochre instead of glowing against the dark page.

Boxes

Only two things on the site sit in boxes. Every box is square, with no border and no shadow, 32px of padding inside, 32px from the next box, and never holds another box. Everything else is separated by hairline rules and space.

Boxed

  • The book: a --sun block, Home only, ink text.
  • Each year's publications: a --paper-raised citation box with the red year inside it. Used on Publications, in Home's recent work, and in a project's related publications.

Not boxed

  • Project cards: a 4px bar, an eyebrow, a title and a line.
  • Poster figures and the portrait: the image, then a caption.
  • Page sections, the masthead, the footer, notes and links.

The raised panels in this document that hold specimens are the document's own furniture, not site components.

Page and image rules

  • One column, no wider than 720px, on reading pages. Publications and Projects widen to 960px and drop back to one column on phones.
  • Square corners on images, cards and buttons, as on printed matter. Rounded ends only on topic tags; the focus ring is the one other rounded thing.
  • Sections are separated by thin rules and space. No shadows.
  • Posters are shown only with Tallersol's permission, each captioned with title, year and source, and the site links out to the UCLA archive rather than copying the collection.
  • Archive images are shown as they are, with no filters. There is one portrait of Richard, on About, and no stock photos anywhere.
  • No icon set. Profile links are plain text, and any link that leaves the site carries a small arrow.

09 — Accessibility

Contrast and the basics

Every text colour is checked against WCAG AA on the grounds it sits on, in light and dark (section 04). The weakest text pairing anywhere is 4.7 : 1. Keyboard focus shows as a 2px poster red outline. Each poster's alt text says what it shows and what it was campaigning for. The site follows the visitor's light or dark setting unless they choose otherwise.

  • One H1 per page, a skip link to the main content, and lang="en-GB" on the page with lang="es" on Spanish names.
  • Every image has an alt text slot. Tap and click targets are at least 24px; the menu button is 44px.
  • The menu and the theme slider work from the keyboard, and with reduced motion the slider's thumb jumps instead of sliding.
  • The page works from 360px up with no sideways scrolling. With JavaScript off, the links show and the slider and filter are hidden.

10 — Register

Decisions & open questions

Settled decisions first, so nobody "fixes" them later. The open items are the ones to settle with Richard before or during the build.

01 — Light and dark, both designedSettled

The site follows the visitor's setting, and a Light / System / Dark slider in the footer lets anyone override it, remembered in that browser only. Dark is a dark paper, not black, and every token has a value for it. The dark inks are deepened rather than lightened (section 04).

02 — One accentSettled

Poster red is the only accent and stays small. Andes blue belongs to the music and memory strand and tags. Sun is a fill, used once, on the book.

03 — History and music onlySettled

The publications list covers the history and music work. Earlier work in other fields isn't listed.

04 — Square, flat, and two boxesSettled

No shadows anywhere, square corners on everything except tags and the focus ring. Sections are separated by rules and space. Only two things sit in boxes: the book (sun) and each year's publications (paper raised). A box never holds another box.

05 — Posters by permissionSettled

A small selection only, with Tallersol's agreement and a credit under each one. The full collection is linked at UCLA, not re-hosted.

06 — Spanish names as writtenSettled

Accents kept (Universidad de Chile, Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile) and marked up with lang="es" so screen readers pronounce them properly.

07 — Phone menuSettled

Below 640px the four links fold behind a burger button; from 640px they sit inline. This replaces v0.1's "no hamburger menu". With JavaScript off the links simply show.

08 — Theme sliderSettled

A three-stop slider (Light, System, Dark) in the footer, System in the middle and the default. Built on radio buttons so keyboard and screen readers work natively; hidden when JavaScript is off.

09 — Publication titlesSettled

Eighteen pixels, regular, in double quotes, as the proposal and v0.1's own specimen showed. The 21px semibold style is for project titles; the book title is that style in italic.

10 — Link arrowSettled

Drawn, not typed: iOS replaces the ↗ character with a blue emoji. It stays on the line with the last word of its link.

11 — Values read backSettled

Done 16 October 2026. The built site was read back against sections 05 to 08: 15 of 16 type rows matched exactly (the 16th, the eyebrow inside the book block, is ink on purpose), and the differences are in the table below. They are now the spec.

12 — Spanish versionOpen

A short Spanish summary on About is planned for launch. Whether the whole site should also be in Spanish is Richard's call; it roughly doubles every future update.

13 — PortraitOpen

One photo of Richard for About. None chosen yet. Natural light, no studio backdrop, no filter. A grey placeholder slot is built.

14 — DomainOpen

Not registered. The favicon and social card wait on it.

15 — Line lengthOpen

The 720px column holds about 90–95 characters at 18px, longer than the 62–70 that v0.1 asked for. The column is kept because the proposal promised it, and short paragraphs (see Voice) carry the load. If long passages read heavy once real text is in, cap paragraphs at about 30em.
